About this tracker
US 2026 Mid-term Election Racebook

This election racebook analyses the 2026 US midterm elections from a uniquely Indian perspective. It highlights the key races that will determine the outcome of these elections, alongside reporting on broader trends in approval ratings, early predictions, primaries, retirement trends, and other legislative dynamics. The racebook also monitors contests involving members of the Indian diaspora and legislators who have been actively shaping India-US ties. While primary statistical data and news reporting are drawn from several credible third-party sources, analysis is drawn from DC Dossier and the publications of researchers at Takshashila Institution's Geostrategy Programme.

Takshashila Institution
Takshashila Institution
Independent Centre for Research & Education in Public Policy · Bengaluru, India

Founded on the conviction that better public policy transforms societies, Takshashila is India's leading independent policy institution. Its Geostrategy Programme produces rigorous, non-partisan analysis on international relations, defence strategy, and Indo-Pacific security — bridging academic research and policy practice. The institution operates a Policy School, publishes the peer-reviewed Indian Public Policy Review, and runs a suite of digital platforms including the All Things Policy podcast. Its work is guided by the values of freedom, openness, tolerance, and pluralism.

Brigadier Anil Raman (Retd.)
Research Fellow
Geostrategy Programme & Defence Fellowship, Takshashila Institution

Brigadier Raman brings over three decades of military experience to his policy work at Takshashila, where he also heads the Defence Fellowship Program. His army career spanned command and staff appointments in disputed border regions and counter-insurgency operations, as well as senior roles at the Army War College — including as a REDFOR analyst and senior instructor. He also completed the higher air command course at the College of Air Warfare.

As a scholar, his attention centres on American domestic politics and foreign policy formation, future conflict paradigms, and military operations across the Indo-Pacific. He was a Visiting Fellow at the US National Defense University, and holds a Master's from the University of Wyoming, where his thesis examined how the Clinton and Bush administrations shaped US foreign policy toward India. His writing appears regularly in The Hindu, The Indian Express, War on the Rocks, and NDTV, covering topics from US–China competition to Iran policy.

Abhishek Kadiyala
Research Analyst
Geostrategy Programme, Takshashila Institution

Abhishek's research sits at the intersection of US domestic politics, the US–India strategic relationship, and the broader dynamics shaping the Indo-Pacific. He curates DC Dossier, a newsletter tracking US congressional legislation as it bears on India, and manages The US Trade Tracker, which maps tariff negotiations and trade policy developments. He is the author of Expeditionary, a historical non-fiction account of the Allied landings in Normandy. Prior to Takshashila, he held research positions at think tanks focused on international security and conflict studies, and worked with UNESCO's OE4BW programme.

He holds a Master's in Intelligence and International Security from King's College London — where he was awarded the Vice Chancellor's India Award — and a Bachelor's in Journalism, International Relations, and Public Policy from St. Joseph's College, Bengaluru.
