Key Policy Areas at Stake

How congressional control shapes the bilateral relationship

Aviation & People-to-People Connectivity

Direct air connectivity between the US and India has expanded under Open Skies agreements. Congressional support for bilateral aviation accords, visa facilitation, and diaspora mobility shapes the depth of people-to-people ties between the two democracies.

Trade & GSP Restoration

India was the largest beneficiary of the Generalised System of Preferences before its expiry in 2020. Renewed reciprocal trade tensions and tariff negotiations hinge on which party controls the House Ways and Means Committee.

Defence Co-production & ITAR

The INDUS-X initiative, GE jet engine transfer, and future co-production deals require congressional notifications and ITAR waiver approvals. Armed Services Committee chairs in both chambers hold significant influence over the pace of these agreements.

Semiconductors & Technology

CHIPS Act implementation, AI governance frameworks, and export control regimes for advanced chips directly affect India's semiconductor ambitions and the commercial technology relationship. The Science and Commerce committees are central.

China Policy & the Quad

Both parties support the Quad framework and treat India as a counterweight to China. However, the depth of security guarantees, intelligence sharing agreements, and the pace of diplomatic engagement vary by administration and congressional posture.

Current Policy Landscape (2025–26)

Live developments in US-India relations shaping the 2026 calculus
Trade
Reciprocal Tariff Standoff
The Trump administration's 26% tariff on Indian goods (paused at 10% for 90 days) has complicated bilateral trade negotiations. India faces pressure to reduce its own tariffs in exchange for a US trade deal — a politically sensitive ask ahead of Indian state elections.
Active Negotiation
Technology
iCET 2.0: Initiative on Critical and Emerging Technology
The US-India iCET framework expanded in 2025 to cover quantum computing, AI safety standards, and space cooperation. CHIPS Act-linked semiconductor cooperation agreement signed; India reciprocated with liberalised FDI rules in chip manufacturing.
Active
Defence
INDUS-X & GE Jet Engine Deal
The GE F414 jet engine technology transfer for India's Tejas Mk2 fighter is proceeding through ITAR congressional notification. INDUS-X has spawned 11 joint defence startup partnerships. Armed Services Committees in both chambers are broadly supportive.
Progressing
Immigration
H-1B & EB Backlog: No Resolution in Sight
The employment-based green card backlog for Indian nationals exceeds 600,000. At current processing rates, a new applicant from India would wait 50–100 years. EAGLE Act has passed the House three times but stalled in the Senate due to per-country cap politics.
Critical
Quad
Quad Summit & Indo-Pacific Framework
The Quad (US, India, Japan, Australia) held its annual summit in 2025 with joint statements on semiconductor supply chains, maritime security, and clean energy. Congressional support is strong across both parties — one of few genuinely bipartisan foreign policy areas.
Bipartisan
Climate
US Withdrawal Risk from Climate Commitments
Republican control of Congress threatens IRA international clean energy provisions and US contributions to the Green Climate Fund, which India has leveraged for renewable energy financing. A Democratic House in 2027 would likely restore these mechanisms.
At Risk

Indian-Americans in the 119th Congress

Six members as of January 2025
The 119th Congress seated six Indian-American members, the highest number in US history. All serve in the House. No Indian-American currently holds a Senate seat, making the 2026 cycle a potential watershed: multiple members are actively campaigning for Senate.
