Key Policy Areas at Stake

How congressional control shapes the bilateral relationship
✈️

H-1B & High-Skilled Immigration

Roughly 70% of H-1B visas go to Indian nationals each year. Congress sets visa caps, employer fee structures, and the backlog clearance mechanism that leaves hundreds of thousands of Indian-born workers waiting decades for green cards.

Critical
📈

Trade & GSP Restoration

India was the largest beneficiary of the Generalised System of Preferences before its expiry in 2020. Renewed reciprocal trade tensions and tariff negotiations hinge on which party controls the House Ways and Means Committee.

Contested
🛡️

Defence Co-production & ITAR

The INDUS-X initiative, GE jet engine transfer, and future co-production deals require congressional notifications and ITAR waiver approvals. Armed Services Committee chairs in both chambers hold significant influence over the pace of these agreements.

Bipartisan
💻

Semiconductors & Technology

CHIPS Act implementation, AI governance frameworks, and export control regimes for advanced chips directly affect India's semiconductor ambitions and the commercial technology relationship. The Science and Commerce committees are central.

Watch
🌎

China Policy & the Quad

Both parties support the Quad framework and treat India as a counterweight to China. However, the depth of security guarantees, intelligence sharing agreements, and the pace of diplomatic engagement vary by administration and congressional posture.

Bipartisan
☀️

Climate & Clean Energy

US-India climate finance, the International Solar Alliance partnership, and critical minerals supply chains depend on whether the Inflation Reduction Act's international clean energy provisions survive congressional scrutiny in a divided government.

Contested

Indian-Americans in the 119th Congress

Six members as of January 2025
The 119th Congress seated six Indian-American members, the highest number in US history. All serve in the House. No Indian-American currently holds a Senate seat, making the 2026 cycle a potential watershed: multiple members are actively campaigning for Senate.
Ami Bera
California 6th District
Democrat House
Longest-serving Indian-American in Congress. Internist and former Sacramento County Chief Medical Officer. Sits on Foreign Affairs and Science committees.
Ro Khanna
California 17th District
Democrat House Senate Bid
Silicon Valley progressive. Announced 2026 California Senate bid. Strong advocate for domestic semiconductor manufacturing and H-1B reform.
Pramila Jayapal
Washington 7th District
Democrat House
Chair of the Congressional Progressive Caucus. Immigrant rights advocate. First Indian-American woman elected to the House.
Raja Krishnamoorthi
Illinois 8th District
Democrat House Senate Bid
Announced 2026 Illinois Senate bid to succeed Dick Durbin. Sits on Intelligence and Oversight committees. National security and anti-corruption focus.
Suhas Subramanyam
Virginia 10th District
Democrat House
Freshman elected in 2024. First Indian-American to represent Virginia in Congress. Former state senator and technology policy adviser.
Shri Thanedar
Michigan 13th District
Democrat House
Entrepreneur and chemist, born in Belgaum, Karnataka. Won a competitive primary in a majority-minority Detroit district. Progressive economics focus.

Indian-American Voter Influence by State

States where the community is large enough to influence competitive races
Indian-Americans vote at higher-than-average rates and are concentrated in suburban districts that have become the most competitive battlegrounds in modern US elections. In several swing states, the community exceeds the margin of victory in recent Senate and presidential races.
